How to Play

Don't say the obvious answer.

Dealer names a prompt and bans the obvious answer. That's the Trap.

You lock a different answer that fits. First lock owns the word. Then guess the Trap — the Bounty.

Tribunal votes out answers that don't fit. One is the Decoy. Vote for it and you lose your survival point.

Points

  • +1 survive the vote · +1 guess the Trap
  • +2 you wrote the Decoy. Votes can't kill it.
  • 0 for the Trap, a vote-out, a timeout, or a Decoy vote. The Bounty still counts.
  • Dealer +1 per Trap hit, +1 per Bounty (max 2).

Two modes

  • Classic — veto as many as you want. More than half the room has to agree.
  • Competitive — one veto. Only a few words can die.
